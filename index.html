Verification Webhook Inspector

A small dev-console view for teams integrating KYC APIs: every verification call with its webhook timeline, a plain-language reason for each failure, and a replay button that shows duplicate deliveries being safely ignored.

I was going through Surepass's KYC APIs and remembered how much time I spent debugging verification callbacks when I built KYC APIs on AWS at 1StopKYC. So I tried a quick prototype of the console I wished I had: one place to see what happened to a request, why it failed, and proof that a retried webhook doesn't get processed twice. It runs on sample data. - Khushboo
